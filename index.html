Six toolkits

Pick the toolkit that fits your question

BioProspector starts with a target molecule and possible routes. GeneCluster starts with candidate genes and genome or transcriptome evidence. Structure Factory covers prediction and design; CryoCore covers experimental maps and model validation.

Biosynthetic routes

BioProspector

Compare biosynthetic routes by evidence, gene count, host fit, and unresolved steps. Outputs Route comparisons, ranked enzyme and gene candidates, follow-up plans. Genome mining

GeneCluster

Find candidate biosynthetic genes across species and compare their genomic neighborhoods. Outputs Candidate-gene tables, genome-context comparisons, reports with sources and evidence gaps. Structural design

Structure Factory

Plan binder design, structure prediction, and screening with comparable inputs and scoring across methods. Outputs Validated stage plans, compute requirements, task packs, candidate-ranking schemas. Cryo-EM

CryoCore

Organize cryo-EM map and model validation, conformational-state comparison, and structural figures. Outputs A local HTML review demo, validation plans, figure specifications. Small-molecule design

Small Molecules

Choose methods for molecular generation, synthesis planning, docking, affinity estimation, and property prediction. Outputs Tool comparisons, setup requirements, separate code, model, and data license records. Fermentation design

Ferm DoE

Generate fermentation experiment designs, analyze supplied results, and plan the next batch within declared constraints. Outputs Design tables, measurement-readiness reports, follow-up batches, scale-transfer reviews.

Get started

Open a toolkit in your agent

  1. Clone one repo and open it in Codex, Claude Code, or another coding agent.
  2. Paste the first request. The agent reads the toolkit's instructions and runs its local example.
  3. Review the outputs, evidence gaps, and dependencies before configuring larger runs.

You set the research question and interpret the evidence. Planning outputs and computational rankings need experimental validation before they support biological claims.
